FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Funngro?

Funngro is positioned as a platform connecting young people with companies and project opportunities. Check the live platform for current eligibility, projects and terms.

What kind of projects can I find?

Examples can include content, promotion, research and other project-based work. Availability changes over time.

Is earning guaranteed?

No. Project availability, selection, completion requirements and payments depend on the specific opportunity and its terms.
